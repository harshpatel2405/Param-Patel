Operators 
---------
Something which performs operations

a                    +                  b
operand1             operatror          operand2

++         a
operator   operand

*******************************************************************
*Types on basis of number of operands*
--------------------------------------
1. Unary Operator -- only single operand 
    . Logical NOT 
    . Increment
    . Decrement

2. Binary Operator -- two operands
    . Arithmetic Operators        +    -    *    /    &
    . Relational Operators        <    >    <=    >=    ==    != 
    . Assignment Operatos         =    +=    -=    *=    /=    %=
    . Logical Operator            AND(&&)    OR(||)
    . Bitwise Operator            &    |    ^    >>    <<

3. Ternary Operator  -- three operands
    used to check mini conditions
    (condition) ? "True" : "False";

*********************************************************************
*Types of Operators*
--------------------
1. Arithmetic Operator
    . +
    . -
    . *
    . /    10 / 3 = 3     10 / 3.0 = 3.3333    (int / int  =  int)
    . %    only works on int    10 % 4    10.5 % 4 X

2. Relational Operator (conditions) (0 - false and 1 - non zero)
    . <       5 < 6 - 1        7 < 6 - 0       6 < 6 - 0
    . >       5 > 6 - 0        7 > 6 - 1       6 > 6 - 0
    . <=      5 <= 6 - 1       7 <= 6 - 0      6 <= 6 - 1
    . >=      5 >= 6 - 0       7 >= 6 - 1      6 >= 6 - 1
    . ==      5 == 6 - 0       7 == 7 - 1
    . !=      5 != 6 - 1       7 != 7 - 0

3. Assignment Operator (Loop)
    . =   (Assignment Operator which assigns value from right to left)
    . +=  sum += i;    -->    sum = sum + i;
    . -=
    . *=
    . /=
    . %=

4. Logical Operator (used to check multiple conditions)
    . &&  (returns true when both the conditions are true )
        -5 && 6
         1 && 1 -> 1

    . ||  (returns true when any of the conditions are true )
    . !   (true -> false , false -> true)

5. Bitwise Operator
    . &
    . |
    . ^
    . >>
    . <<

6. Increment / Decrement (pre / post)
    . ++ -> increase by 1
        count++  -> count = count + 1;

    . -- -> deacrese by 1
        count-- -> count = count - 1;


    . post
        -> first done and complete the task assigned and then updates 
        a = 15;
        c = 5 + a++; // 5 + 15
        printf("A : %d", a); // 16
        printf("C : %d", c); // 20

    .pre 
        -> first updates and then does the assigned tasks
        a = 15;
        c = 5 + ++a; // 5 + 16
        printf("A : %d", a); // 16
        printf("C : %d", c); // 21



7. Special Operator
    . sizeof() -> returns size of datatype / variable in bytes 
    . &        -> ampersand (address of)
