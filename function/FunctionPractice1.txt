--------------------------------
1) WITHOUT RETURN, WITHOUT ARGUMENT
--------------------------------
1. Print your name.  
2. Print "Hello, World!" 10 times.  
3. Print first 10 natural numbers.  
4. Display multiplication table of 5.  
5. Print all even numbers between 1 and 50.  
6. Print sum of first 100 natural numbers.  
7. Print squares of numbers from 1 to 10.  

--------------------------------
2) WITHOUT RETURN, WITH ARGUMENT
--------------------------------

1. Accept a number and print its square.  
2. Accept a number and print whether it is even or odd.  
3. Accept two numbers and print their sum.  
4. Accept two numbers and print their product.  
5. Accept a number and display its multiplication table.  
6. Accept two numbers and print the greater one.
